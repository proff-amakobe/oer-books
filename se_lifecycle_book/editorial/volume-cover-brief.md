# Two-Volume Cover Brief

Final covers are intentionally deferred until the new ISBNs and Ingram templates are available.

## Shared Series System

Both covers should clearly belong to *The Complete Software Engineering Lifecycle*: consistent title hierarchy, typography, grid, series line, author treatment, publisher treatment, and volume marker. Each cover must be built from its own final Ingram template after ISBN assignment.

## Volume I — Software Engineering Foundations and Design

- Themes: requirements, modeling, architecture, UI/UX, development workflow, version control, and testing.
- Palette: deep navy, royal blue, cyan, white, and a restrained accent.
- Visual direction: structured models, architectural relationships, and a progression from problem definition to verified design.

## Volume II — Software Delivery, Operations, and Evolution

- Themes: CI/CD, data, APIs, cloud, deployment, security, operations, maintenance, and evolution.
- Palette: deep navy, cyan, academic blue, and restrained crimson.
- Visual direction: delivery pipelines, connected production systems, operational feedback, protection, and continuous evolution.

No existing complete-edition ISBN, barcode, spine width, or cover template may be reused.
Do not create production covers until the standalone ISBNs have been assigned and official Ingram templates have been generated from the final page counts.
