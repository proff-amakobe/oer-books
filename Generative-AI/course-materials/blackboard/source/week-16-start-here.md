# Week 16 Start Here: Module Overview

Welcome to Week 16. This week focuses on **Final Project Demonstration, Portfolio Synthesis, and Reflection** and moves your AI-powered research-assistant portfolio toward **Final Integrated Portfolio & Demonstration**. Keep the same domain and target user in view: the purpose is to make a connected design decision, not to collect an isolated technique.

Start with your project charter, the Chapter 15 wrap-up, and your existing portfolio. There is no major new companion reading requirement; the optional selection is a context resource. Note the assumptions behind one claim and what evidence would make it useful for your assistant. The written module guide gives the full prompts and rubrics.

Your first assignment is the discussion, **What Changed in Your Design — and Why?**. Use the initial post to defend a decision, compare an alternative, and identify a meaningful limitation. Respond to two peers with analysis that extends their reasoning. The second assignment, **Integrated Project Portfolio**, tests your judgment against all fifteen revised artifacts. The third assignment asks you to demonstrate and defend the integrated design.

Pay special attention to the difference between evidence and assumption. Preserve outputs or sources where needed, label projected behavior, and explain how your decision serves the user. Show how evidence changed your design and where uncertainty remains; a live prototype is optional.

Plan approximately 6–8 hours across the week. The guide’s day groups suggest pacing; Blackboard displays the actual due dates. Before submitting, review every rubric, check your files, and retain the editable work for your final project record.
