### Assignment 1 — Ethical Is Not the Same as Legal — Why Does That Matter?

> [SUBMISSION] Type: Discussion Board. Points: 50. Submit one initial post and two peer responses in the Week 14 Discussion item. Use Blackboard’s displayed due dates.

Write an initial post of approximately **350–500 words**. Make a defensible claim, compare alternatives, and use evidence from the assigned material. Connect your judgment to your research-assistant design rather than summarizing the chapter.

#### Discussion Prompt

**Part A — Position:** Describe a deployment context and jurisdiction in which ethical approval alone would not establish legal readiness.

**Part B — Comparison:** Distinguish an applicable obligation, a plausible but uncertain obligation, and a framework that does not apply; support the distinction with an official source.

**Part C — Judgment:** Recommend a governance action and identify a question requiring qualified review instead of asserting legal certainty.

#### Peer Response Guidance

Reply to **two classmates, 150–200 words each**. Challenge an assumption, introduce a counterexample, identify an overlooked trade-off, ask a substantive question, or connect the design to another course concept. Agreement, praise, or summary alone is insufficient. Explain why your contribution matters to the peer’s decision.
