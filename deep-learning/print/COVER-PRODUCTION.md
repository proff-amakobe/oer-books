# Commercial Cover Production Requirements

The existing cover in `assets/cover.svg` and `assets/cover.png` is the digital front cover. It is not a printer-ready wraparound cover.

A commercial wraparound cover must not be produced until all of the following are final:

- interior page count after proofreading and printer preflight;
- trim size and bleed specification from the selected printer;
- paper stock, paper weight, and resulting pages-per-inch value;
- binding method;
- printer-specific spine-width formula and safety tolerances;
- barcode and assigned print ISBN requirements;
- color-space and PDF standard required by the printer.

The wraparound file will require a back cover, spine, and front cover positioned within the printer's exact template. Spine width must be calculated from the final manufacturing specification, not estimated from the current PDF. The Phase 3 deliverable is therefore the no-bleed print interior only.
